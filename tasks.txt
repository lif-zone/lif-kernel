reg.js: open questions (to return to at the end of the filelist work)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- double request on a stored failure: a fetch in the forced cache mode
  (a filelist, a url of the hash table) that is not 200 is asked again from
  the cdn (cache: 'reload'). jsdelivr answers 403 for the filelist of a
  repository of more than 50MB (lif-os) and marks it cacheable for a year:
  chrome answers the first fetch from its cache, and the retry is a real
  request, at every start of the kernel. Suggested: remove the retry, and
  with opt.no_cache fetch a failed filelist with cache: 'reload'.
- enable_hash_validation is 0. With 1 the hashes that the filelist gives
  are computed from the content and compared.
- page_max of the gitlab.com full list is 50 pages (5,000 entries): a
  larger repository has no filelist from it.
- nothing passes opt.no_cache to reg_get() yet: kernel.js uses its
  refresh_tm.
- the unpkg.com entry of the file contents (reg_cdn.npm.src) has the key
  u: and is off. Only its src_files entry is on.
- a cdn that failed is skipped for 3 seconds: filelists of other github.com
  packages asked in that time go to api.github.com (60 requests per hour
  per ip without a login).
- git_ver_resolve() (kernel.js) is to move into reg.js, by src_ver of
  reg_cdn (#6).
- a content that was fetched under a filelist of sha256 (jsdelivr, unpkg)
  is not found by a filelist of git_sha1 (gitlab.com, api.github.com), and
  the other way: only the hash kind of the filelist is in the hash table,
  and sha256.
